RED WIND LINEHAUL
'YOUR GOODS IN GOOD HANDS'
----------------------------
Got a contract from a major client who needs a skilled pilot to
move a large load of luxury items from Orbituary above Pyro III to
Teasa Spaceport in Lorville.
You'll need room for at least 8 SCU cargo containers in your hold.
You'll also need a handheld tractor beam to get everything loaded
and unloaded.
And as a fair warning, the things you'll be carrying are pricey
enough that you might attract some unwanted attention. Be ready
to defend yourself if you can't evade.
Reputation Awarded: 200
